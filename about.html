Humare Baare Mein

Harsha Dairy ki shuruaat Bidhuna, Auraiya mein shuddh aur bina milawat ka doodh har ghar tak pahunchane ke ek nek lakshya ke sath ki gayi hai.

Mera naam Harshbardhan Singh hai. B.Sc. Agriculture mein apni padhai poori karne ke baad, maine aadhunik (modern) smart farming aur agriculture ki behtareen takneekon ko dairy business mein laane ka faisla kiya. Padhai ke dauran sikhi gayi baaton ka istemal karke hum pashuon ke acche swasthya aur chare ka khayal rakhte hain.

Hamara vishwas hai ki baccho ki achi sehat ke liye shuddh doodh se badhkar kuch nahi. Isliye hum Milk ATM machine jaisi aadhunik suvidhaon ke madhyam se aapke ghar tak bilkul taaza aur healthy doodh seedhe farm se pahunchate hain.
