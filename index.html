⚠️ Doodh aur Khadya Padarthon mein Milawat Ek Gambhir Khatra!

Harsha Dairy ka mukhya uddesya Bidhuna mein isi zehreeli milawat ko jad se samapt karna hai. Akhbar ki reports ke mutabik khadya padarthon mein milawat se hamare swasthya par bahut bura asar pad raha hai:

Akhbar ki Mukhya Batein (Report Highlights):

  • Laakhon mein namoone, hazaron mein fail: FSSAI ki report ke mutabik desh bhar mein lakhon khadya namoonon ki jaanch hui jismein hazaaron tay manako par khare nahi utre, aur milawat ke mamle mein Uttar Pradesh sabse aage raha hai.
  • Gambhir bimariyan: Doosrit bhojan aur milawati doodh se 200 se adhik prakar ki gambhir bimariyan hone ka khatra rehta hai.
  • Chote bachon par sabse bada jokhim: 5 saal se kam umr ke bachon par doosrit bhojan ka jokhim sabhi se zyada hota hai, jisse unke mastishk (brain) ke vikas par bura asar padta hai.
  • Harsha Dairy ka Sankalp: Isi milawati vyavastha ko badalne aur aapke parivar ko 100% shuddhata dene ke liye hum Milk ATM le kar aaye hain, taaki aapko mile sirf pure milk aur pure trust!
